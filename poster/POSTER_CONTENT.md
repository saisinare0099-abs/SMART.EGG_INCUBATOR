# Smart Automatic Egg Incubator — Poster Content Draft

## Problem
Maintaining a suitable and stable temperature during egg incubation requires regular monitoring.

## Proposed Solution
A microcontroller-based prototype that monitors internal temperature and controls a relay-operated heating arrangement, with fan-based air circulation.

## Key Features
- Arduino Nano-based control (reported; verify final board)
- DHT22 temperature sensing (verify final installed sensor)
- I2C LCD display
- Relay-based heater switching
- Side-mounted 12 V DC circulation fan
- Buzzer alert (verify final alert logic)
